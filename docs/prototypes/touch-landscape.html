Pattern actions

This is an isolated interaction model, not the Android app. “Play” moves the cursor without sound. Listen mode demonstrates held-note feedback only. Nothing reads or writes your ThorTracker projects.

Modeled here: cell selection, note/OFF/clear entry, per-lane sound choices, one effect slot, Undo/Redo and visual collapse. Arrangement, file operations and range selection are covered in the proposal, not simulated here.
