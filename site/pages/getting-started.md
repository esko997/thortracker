## Save and export

Keep a named **`.thor` project** as the editable master. [Saving and recovery](/files/#saving-and-recovery) explains external saves, automatic recovery and the single previous-project slot. [Projects & exports](/files/) lists delivery formats, conversion reports and [WAV rendering](/files/#export-to-wav).

For a chip's instruments, effects, shared resources and export restrictions, use the [sound-system references](/systems/). If a note is silent, check [channel and instrument compatibility](/troubleshooting/#a-note-or-channel-is-silent).
