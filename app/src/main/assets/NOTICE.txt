Game Boy playback uses hUGEDriver 6.1.3 (public domain) and binjgb at commit
16621111ed0ee73bcc45c912a823bcebedcffc0f (MIT, notice below).
The binjgb audio output buffer is locally adapted to floating-point samples.

NES playback uses the FamiStudio sound engine 4.5.3 (notice below; its NTSC note
tables are part of FamiStudio, MIT, notice below), the m6502.h 6502 emulator from
floooh/chips at commit 9e88298ce56319953ac7a43213a1120359f7a3a6 (zlib, notice below)
and blip_buf 1.1.0 by Shay Green (GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later;
source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/blip_buf with its license.txt). The 2A03 APU
emulation is Thor Tracker's own code (GPL-3.0-or-later).
