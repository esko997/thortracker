Game Boy playback uses hUGEDriver 6.1.3 (public domain) and binjgb at commit
16621111ed0ee73bcc45c912a823bcebedcffc0f (MIT, notice below).
The binjgb audio output buffer is locally adapted to floating-point samples.

NES playback uses the FamiStudio sound engine 4.5.3 (notice below; its NTSC note
tables, for NES + VRC6 songs its VRC6 build's saw note tables and for NES + VRC7
songs its VRC7 build's note tables are part of FamiStudio, MIT, notice below), the
m6502.h 6502 emulator from floooh/chips at commit
9e88298ce56319953ac7a43213a1120359f7a3a6 (zlib, notice below) and blip_buf 1.1.0
by Shay Green (GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later; source in
app/src/main/cpp/third_party/blip_buf with its license.txt). The 2A03 APU and VRC6
audio emulation are Thor Tracker's own code (GPL-3.0-or-later). The VRC7's FM
synthesis is emulated by emu2413 1.6.4 by Mitsutaka Okazaki (MIT, notice below;
source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/emu2413 with its LICENSE,
github.com/digital-sound-antiques/emu2413 at commit
a1d5120903b75f93d3eb7690e34a29c0b4add903).

Commodore 64 SID playback uses the GoatTracker 2.77 playroutine by Lasse Oorni
(Cadaver), which is not under the GPL and may be used freely (notice below), the
same m6502.h, and reSID 1.0 by Dag Lem (GNU General Public License 2 or later;
source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/resid-1.0 with its COPYING, AUTHORS and
README, github.com/daglem/reSID at commit 02c456abfe23cc4f06d94424e40beab8c90fa31f).
Thor Tracker adds a configured siddefs.h and a C interface (sid/sid_resid.cpp).

OPL3 (YMF262) FM playback uses Thor Tracker's own player (GPL-3.0-or-later) on
Nuked-OPL3 1.8 by Nuke.YKT (GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later, notice
below; source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/nuked-opl3 with its LICENSE,
github.com/nukeykt/Nuked-OPL3 at commit 765ec962e473aeb767e4cba74ffdc8f588ffbfe8),
resampled with the same blip_buf.

The Mega Drive (YM2612) system runs SGDK's XGM Z80 sound driver by Stephane
Dallongeville (MIT, notice below; source in third_party/sgdk-xgm, SGDK commit
9a78a44143a867cd8c5053b95cd061a21f0b73c2) on the Z80 emulator z80.h by Andre
Weissflog (zlib, the chips notice above), with the YM2612 emulated by ymfm by
Aaron Giles (BSD-3-Clause, notice below; app/src/main/cpp/third_party/ymfm at
commit 81aec25ccbb98f4873a255f7551ac4dadac59b4a); its XGM export ports SGDK
xgmtool's conversion (MIT).

WonderSwan and WonderSwan Color playback uses Thor Tracker's own player
(GPL-3.0-or-later) on a C port of the WonderSwan APU of ares (ISC, notice below;
ares/ws/apu at github.com/ares-emulator/ares commit
038bcb91c230d33fc8911e2bc21b53c66d70a2b0, ported into app/src/main/cpp/ws/ws_apu.c
with documented changes following the WSdev wiki's hardware documentation, CC0),
resampled with the same blip_buf.
