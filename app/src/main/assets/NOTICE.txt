Game Boy playback uses hUGEDriver 6.1.3 (public domain) and binjgb at commit
16621111ed0ee73bcc45c912a823bcebedcffc0f (MIT, notice below).
The binjgb audio output buffer is locally adapted to floating-point samples.

NES playback uses the FamiStudio sound engine 4.5.3 (notice below; its NTSC note
tables, for NES + VRC6 songs its VRC6 build's saw note tables, for NES + VRC7
songs its VRC7 build's note tables, for NES + FDS songs its FDS note tables, and
for NES + N163 songs its N163 note tables for 1-8 channels, are part of
FamiStudio, MIT, notice below), the m6502.h 6502
emulator from floooh/chips at commit 9e88298ce56319953ac7a43213a1120359f7a3a6
(zlib, notice below) and blip_buf 1.1.0 by Shay Green (GNU Lesser General Public
License 2.1 or later; source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/blip_buf with its
license.txt). The 2A03 APU, VRC6, FDS, MMC5, Namco 163 and Sunsoft 5B audio emulation are
Thor Tracker's own code (GPL-3.0-or-later). The Namco 163 was written from the
NESdev wiki page "Namco 163 audio"; the Sunsoft 5B from the NESdev wiki with
emu2149's documented behaviour as a reference (no emu2149 code is included).
The VRC7's FM synthesis is emulated by emu2413 1.6.4 by Mitsutaka Okazaki
(MIT, notice below; source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/emu2413 with its LICENSE,
github.com/digital-sound-antiques/emu2413 at commit
a1d5120903b75f93d3eb7690e34a29c0b4add903).
Thor Tracker's Sunsoft 5B build adds a scoped $5F instrument-load opcode to keep
the shared manual envelope period when switching between identical instrument
copies. The generator applies this modification to a temporary copy of the
pinned engine source; the vendored FamiStudio source is unchanged.
The FDS emulation was written from the NESdev wiki page "FDS audio". Its DAC
levels are the 2C33 measurements by plgDavid (cleaned up by Persune) as tabulated
in FamiStudio 4.5.3's ThirdParty/NesSndEmu/nes_apu/Nes_Fds.cpp (Nes_Snd_Emu,
GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later).
Thor Tracker's FDS build corrects the instrument loader's ambiguity between
manual modulation speeds and automatic modulation, using a tagged high byte.
It also adds a scoped $5F instrument-load opcode to retain runtime modulation
while selecting the actual bank slot for legato notes and live edits.
These patches apply only to a temporary copy of the pinned engine source.

Commodore 64 SID playback uses the GoatTracker 2.77 playroutine by Lasse Oorni
(Cadaver), which is not under the GPL and may be used freely (notice below), the
same m6502.h, and reSID 1.0 by Dag Lem (GNU General Public License 2 or later;
source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/resid-1.0 with its COPYING, AUTHORS and
README, github.com/daglem/reSID at commit 02c456abfe23cc4f06d94424e40beab8c90fa31f).
Thor Tracker adds a configured siddefs.h and a C interface (sid/sid_resid.cpp).

OPL3 (YMF262) FM playback uses Thor Tracker's own player (GPL-3.0-or-later) on
Nuked-OPL3 1.8 by Nuke.YKT (GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later, notice
below; source in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/nuked-opl3 with its LICENSE,
github.com/nukeykt/Nuked-OPL3 at commit 765ec962e473aeb767e4cba74ffdc8f588ffbfe8),
resampled with the same blip_buf.

The Mega Drive (YM2612) system runs SGDK's XGM Z80 sound driver by Stephane
Dallongeville (MIT, notice below; source in third_party/sgdk-xgm, SGDK commit
9a78a44143a867cd8c5053b95cd061a21f0b73c2) on the Z80 emulator z80.h by Andre
Weissflog (zlib, the chips notice above), with the YM2612 emulated by ymfm by
Aaron Giles (BSD-3-Clause, notice below; app/src/main/cpp/third_party/ymfm at
commit 81aec25ccbb98f4873a255f7551ac4dadac59b4a); its XGM export ports SGDK
xgmtool's conversion (MIT).

NES + EPSM songs run the FamiStudio sound engine 4.5.3 built with its EPSM support
(with FamiStudio's EPSM note tables, MIT, notice below) on the same m6502.h, and
emulate the EPSM's Yamaha YMF288 (OPN3-L: FM, SSG and rhythm) with the same ymfm
by Aaron Giles (BSD-3-Clause, notice below; its ymf288 class).
Thor Tracker applies scoped corrections to a temporary copy of that driver:
the FM patch loader writes LFO settings to global register $22, an SSG attack
exits before it can overwrite rhythm pan state, and a $5F SSG instrument-load
extension preserves legato envelope positions and runtime periods while
retaining the actual instrument slot for live edits.
The Yamaha YM2608 rhythm ROM (the 8 KB of ADPCM-A drum samples the rhythm channels
play) is NOT included in Thor Tracker, its source, its builds, its demo songs or
anything it exports. Users who own a copy import it themselves; Thor Tracker only
checks it (size and SHA-256) and keeps it in its private storage on that device.
